# Khlo independent visual-direction review

## Review scope

Khlo independently reviewed the three Issue #497 frames for originality, founder identity, spatial-intelligence coherence, operational focus, truth, and decision gravity. This was not a wording-only review.

## Disposition

### 1. Sovereign Decision Chamber — PASS

The strongest and most original direction. Parcel geometry communicates the consequence of the decision instead of decorating a form. The selected parcel, branching territorial paths, recommendation, and explicit choices form one coherent decision environment.

Non-blocking refinement: make the exposure difference between green and blue futures even more immediate, and avoid drifting into a strategy-game aesthetic.

### 2. Live Cadastral Field — PASS

The county becomes the operating surface. The frame has one dominant focus, truthful actor attribution, restrained density, and substantially less dashboard-template character than the rejected prototype.

Non-blocking refinement: connect `BLOCKED / CONTAINED` more directly to the active outcome and integrate landed outcomes more spatially.

### 3. Civic Geospatial Instrument — NEEDS_REMEDIATION

The current-state truth defects are closed: `No successor is authorized` and `NO ACTION REQUIRED` are now explicit. The survey-sheet language is promising, but the frame still uses several competing rectangular panels. It lacks one dominant focal point and retains too much enterprise/editorial composition to advance as the primary layout.

## Ranked recommendation

Advance one coherent system:

1. Use **Live Cadastral Field** as the normal operating environment.
2. Transform that same environment into **Sovereign Decision Chamber** only when owner authority is genuinely required.
3. Borrow **Civic Geospatial Instrument** typography and evidence notation selectively, without its floating-panel composition.

```text
FIELD: PASS
INSTRUMENT_TRUTH: PASS
INSTRUMENT_COMPOSITION: NON-ADVANCING
CHAMBER: PASS
RECOMMENDED_SYSTEM: FIELD + CHAMBER
PRODUCTION_IMPLEMENTATION_APPROVED: false
```
