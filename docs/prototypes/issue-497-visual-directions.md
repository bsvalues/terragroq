# Issue #497 TerraFusion visual directions

## Status

`FOUNDER_DISPOSITION: REJECTED_FOUNDER_DESIGN_IDENTITY`

The first prototype remains rejected and unapproved for implementation. These frames are three separate visual hypotheses, not incremental revisions and not production architecture.

## Direction 01: Live Cadastral Field

**Thesis:** The county is the interface.

The operating outcome is anchored directly to a living parcel field. Current execution, owner attention, next action, and landed outcomes behave like spatial signals rather than dashboard sections. Dark mineral contrast and precise parcel boundaries make the focal outcome dominant without turning the screen into a generic mission-control HUD.

## Direction 02: Civic Geospatial Instrument

**Thesis:** Evidence becomes civic machinery.

This is a bright, survey-grade instrument rather than a soft institutional dashboard. Cadastral geometry provides the grid. A selected parcel, evidence path, and exact operating readout combine the feeling of a legal record, field instrument, and modern government system.

## Direction 03: Sovereign Decision Chamber

**Thesis:** Decisions reveal spatial consequence.

The decision is the environment. Two spatial futures emerge from one selected parcel, and each choice states its actual consequence. Recommendation, risk, and options carry visual gravity without becoming a conventional form or a pair of abstract buttons.

## Truth and scope

- Atlas process-host foundation is shown as complete, verified, merged, and bounded-authority consumed.
- No runtime adoption, deployment, Atlas mutation, or cutover is implied.
- The operating state explicitly says no owner action is required.
- No successor is represented as authorized; the assessor workflow is a proposal.
- The decision state uses explicit choices: `Approve single-parcel first` and `Request a batch-first plan`.
- Choosing a direction explicitly authorizes neither implementation nor production change.
- All data is static mock content for Issue #497 founder review.
- No production Home behavior, queue, Goal Console, Hermes, authority, database, or runtime state is read or changed.
- PR #498 must remain draft and unmerged until the founder selects a direction.
