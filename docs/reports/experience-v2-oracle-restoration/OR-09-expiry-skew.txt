THE ROUTE READS GRANT EXPIRY THROUGH THE WRONG CLOCK
Measured on HERMES 2026-08-25, against the live GRANT-0019 row.

`lib/db/schema.ts` stores authority timestamps through a custom `utcWallTimestamp` type whose
`fromDriver` explicitly undoes node-pg's local-time interpretation, so anything reading the registry
through drizzle gets the intended instant.

`app/api/system/node/stamp-identity/route.ts` does not read through drizzle. Its authority leg uses
the raw `pg` pool and does `new Date(row.expiresAt)`, which bypasses the custom type entirely -- so a
stored UTC wall clock is interpreted as LOCAL time. HERMES is UTC-7.

The grant was written to expire two hours after it was created. The route considers it live for nine.

Direction matters: west of UTC a grant outlives its bound, east of UTC it dies early. Either way the
number in the record is not the number being enforced.

NOT REPAIRED HERE, deliberately. The fix edits `route.ts`, which is one of the thirteen modules the
settlement driver digest-pins -- changing it mid-proof would invalidate this lane's own evidence
chain, and it is outside the owner's authorised sequence. Typed CONT-EXPV2-GRANT-EXPIRY-TZ-SKEW.

It is also why GRANT-0019 was REVOKED after its single authorised use rather than left to lapse:
revocation is checked by status, not by clock, so it is the only bound here that behaves as written.
