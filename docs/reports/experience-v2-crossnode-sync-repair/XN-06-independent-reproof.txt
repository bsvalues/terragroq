LC-08b — WHY ATLAS HOLDS 70 ARCHIVES AND HERMES HOLDS 40
---------------------------------------------------------

Noticed while reading LC-08 and chased rather than assumed:

  LOCAL: 40   REMOTE: 70   LOCAL_MISSING_ON_ATLAS: 0   ATLAS_ONLY: 30

Every local archive is on ATLAS. The 30 extras are older files:

  2026-08-11+11:00:04  hermes_pgdata-20260807_075819.tar.gz
  2026-08-11+11:00:05  hermes_pgdata-20260807_080601.tar.gz
  2026-08-11+11:00:05  hermes_pgdata-20260808_030002.tar.gz
  ...
  2026-08-25+10:42:52  terrafusion_..._redis_data-20260825_030003.tar.gz

The cause is precise and benign: `scp` does not preserve mtime, so a file's ATLAS-side
retention clock starts when it was COPIED, not when it was created. Both sides prune at
14 days, but they are counting from different zeroes, so off-box copies survive roughly
four days longer than their local originals. The asymmetry runs in the safe direction —
the off-box copy outlives the local one — and is a property of the design, not a defect.
Typed as an observation only; nothing was changed for it.
