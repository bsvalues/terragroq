=== the finding ===
The deployed runtime's credential authenticates and reads the authority registry. What it cannot do
is REACH it, because .env.local names 192.168.88.5 and ATLAS moved to .8 on 2026-08-25.

So the wall in front of the deployed cockpit is CONT-EXPV2-RESOLVER-NOT-WIRED, not
CONT-EXPV2-RUNTIME-CREDENTIAL-STALE. The register said the two had to be closed together and that
closing the resolver alone "would replace a VISIBLE wrong address with an INVISIBLE wrong
credential". Measured, the credential underneath was never wrong for this process, so closing the
resolver closes the whole thing.

Two failure modes look identical from a distance and are not: a connection REFUSED at the transport
and a connection REJECTED at authentication. OR-13 saw 28P01 because the settlement driver it was
debugging sourced its credential from a different file than the deployed service does.

=== what was NOT done, and why ===
No password was read, printed, copied, changed, or moved between files by this lane. The live
.env.local was never written: its SHA256 is AD51C4BEBBF797284D83237E1D9EEF778B6FD57A026FDDDA14ADFCB10B67CF20
before the deploy and the same after it. The repair was to resolve the ADDRESS at boot, which needs
no access to the credential at all.
