=== why no grant was minted ===
The continuation packet authorises one re-mint to finish the acceptance test. The acceptance test
cannot be finished, because no session can be minted for the owner identity (JC-03). Three reasons
not to mint anyway, in increasing order of seriousness:

1. A grant exists to authorise a specific run. With no run possible, minting produces standing
   A3_WRITE_SHARED permission under #995 with nothing to spend it on -- the exact state
   GRANT-0019's own revokeReason says must not outlive the proof.

2. The window would be real. With the timezone defect fixed, a two-hour grant now expires in two
   hours rather than nine -- but a grant minted now and left unused while waiting for the owner is a
   live permission sitting in the registry for however long that takes.

3. The canonical recorder REFUSES it, by design. PR #1008's review found that
   OR-01-record-grant.mjs's replay guard was written on `grantCovers`, and coverage is exactly what
   revocation removes -- so after GRANT-0019 was revoked, a re-run would have minted a fresh ACTIVE
   grant with no owner decision behind it. #1008 called that the worst defect in the lane and
   rewrote the guard to test for the issuance's existence IN ANY STATUS, before --record is
   consulted, with no override flag. Minting here would mean weakening that guard.

Point 3 is decisive on its own and is the reason this is a report rather than an attempt. Removing
a safety rail that a previous lane installed, in order to hit a step in my own packet, would be the
worst thing this lane could do.
